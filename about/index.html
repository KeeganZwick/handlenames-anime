About this site

Last updated: 3 September 2026

The Anime Handle Generator is a free tool that helps you come up with a Japanese-style name for an anime OC, a fan persona, a Discord handle, a Twitter / TikTok / Instagram / Steam / GitHub username, or just for fun. The site is part of anime.gethandlenames.com, a small collection of name and handle tools.

What it does

The Generator combines real Japanese vocabulary and cultural data to produce names that look and feel like genuine Japanese names, without ripping off specific copyrighted characters. It offers four flavors:

  • Cute / animal — animal and nature words from JMdict and JLPT community word lists, with optional English prefixes ("Cute", "Little", "Sweet", etc.) for kawaii-feel pairings like CuteNeko.
  • Fusion — pairs a word you type (or a random word from the page's language) with a Japanese word, formatted however you like (CamelCase, spaced, hyphenated, etc.).
  • IRL Names — full names built from real Japanese given-names and surnames registries.
  • Mythology — real Shinto shrines, Buddhist temples, mythological figures, and historical martial terms, with a built-in "pair with a given name" mode.

Where the data comes from

All output is generated from real public-domain or openly-licensed sources:

  • JMdict — James William Breen's Japanese-Multilingual dictionary, used for the animal and nature word pool.
  • JLPT community word lists — vocabulary tagged by JLPT level, contributed by volunteers under MIT.
  • Wiktionary — for vocabulary-as-given-names (Midori, Aoi, Sora, etc.).
  • Japanese given-names and surname registries — publicly available lists used in the IRL Names tab.
  • Wikipedia — for shrines, temples, and mythological figures. All entries are real cultural sites and real kami / heroes, not derived from any specific copyrighted character.

Full credit list: see ATTRIBUTIONS.md.

What the site doesn't do

  • It does not copy or remix any specific anime / manga / game / light-novel character names. We deliberately stay clear of franchise IP.
  • It does not require an account, does not store generated names on a server, and does not collect personal data beyond what the browser analytics and ads described in the Privacy Policy capture.
  • It does not make any guarantee of uniqueness — please check that your chosen name is actually available on the platform you want to use it on (each result has a built-in availability-check menu).
